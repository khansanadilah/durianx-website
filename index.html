01Definitions

API means Application Programming Interface, being a set of protocols and instructions that facilitate interconnection between systems.

Digital Financial Asset Trader or DFAT Provider means a centralized Digital Financial Asset exchange and/or Digital Financial Asset trader that is fully licensed and supervised by OJK to conduct DFAT Services in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.

DFAT Services mean Digital Financial Asset trading services provided by a DFAT Provider which includes the following activities:

a.facilitation of the sale and/or purchase of Digital Financial Asset against Indonesian Rupiah currency through a DFAT Provider’s licensed trading system;
b.facilitation of the exchange between one or more types of Digital Financial Assets listed in the official Digital Financial Asset register;
c.custody or storage of Digital Financial Asset, provided exclusively by a DFAT Provider or its duly licensed and approved custody partners;
d.transfer or movement of Digital Financial Asset between Wallets within a DFAT Provider’s licensed ecosystem; and
e.other Digital Financial Asset–related services that are explicitly permitted under prevailing laws and regulations and approved by OJK.
